name: Zero Strategy dynasty and relationship rules
description: Persistent rules for births, relationships, succession, country inheritance, and dynasty surnames.
---
Every recorded child must have exactly two recorded parents who are mutually married or in a love relationship. Marriage, births, and lineage tracking must work under every government form; tribal and tyrannical rulers can pass power to children. If a sitting ruler inherits another country, that ruler's former country is absorbed into the new realm. Active country and nomad offices must only point to living leaders, including after save migration; keep death history intact. Living dynasties use distinct, human-readable surnames, not numeric IDs. Each new culture receives a randomly selected, persisted full-name structure and a fixed surname prefix or ending; surname stems are built from random syllables, not real-world surname lists. Names should follow their assigned structure and use parent data for patronymics or maternal/paternal surnames. The ruling dynasty is only the current ruler's family; the country's living-character list includes every named living resident, regardless of current or former dynasty, and must stay separate from the ruling-dynasty view. Primogeniture follows birth order and should not skip an eligible dynast merely because they live in another country. For ages below 66, calculate an annual death chance whose compounded probability over the full age band equals the design's stated period risk; only elderly characters use the design's separate annual risk.

**Why:** the user explicitly required these rules for Zero Strategy, including the living-office and preserved-biography invariant.

**How to apply:** enforce the relationship invariant in birth/event resolution and save migration; repair dead office holders through existing succession rules without deleting their person records, and route country successions through foreign-ruler annexation; preserve descendant eligibility across government changes; order primogeniture branches by birth date and include foreign dynasts; keep surname identity stable by dynasty; show all living named country residents separately from the current ruler's family and link surnames to the dynasty genealogy; preserve each culture's name structure and fixed affix in saves and migration; use `1 - (1 - periodRisk)^(1 / yearsInBand)` for each annual risk below age 66.
